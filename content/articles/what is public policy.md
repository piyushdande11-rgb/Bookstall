Public policy shapes every aspect of our daily lives, from the quality of the air we breathe to the funding of our schools. But what exactly is it?

In simple terms, public policy is a system of laws, regulatory measures, courses of action, and funding priorities concerning a given topic promulgated by a governmental entity or its representatives.

### Why Students Should Care

When students engage with public policy, they transition from passive observers of society to active participants in its creation. Understanding local governance allows young leaders to:
- Advocate for better educational resources.
- Protect local environmental assets.
- Ensure their community's voice is heard at the municipal level.

At Learning Beyond the Classroom, we believe that civic education is the first step toward lasting societal change.
